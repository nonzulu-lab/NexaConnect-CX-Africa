About NexaConnect CX Africa

Professional customer experience support from Johannesburg, South Africa.

NexaConnect CX Africa is a South African customer experience and business process outsourcing provider supporting local and international businesses.


We provide flexible outsourced solutions designed for businesses that need reliable customer support without the cost and complexity of building a large in-house contact centre.


Our model allows clients to start small and scale their support services as their business grows.
